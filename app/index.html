Willow House — a household budget that lives where the family lives. Envelopes, bills, goals and reports; every figure comes from the ledger on this page.

All data stays in this browser (localStorage). No accounts, no tracking, no network requests — the site works fully offline. Demo household: Maya & Dan Alvarez, Sofia & Leo, November 2026.
